# Pulmonary nodule
aliases: lung nodule, solitary pulmonary nodule, coin lesion, granuloma
source: https://radiopaedia.org/articles/pulmonary-nodule

A pulmonary nodule is a rounded opacity up to 3 cm in diameter surrounded by lung; a larger lesion is called a mass. Many nodules seen on radiographs are benign, for example calcified granulomas from previous infection.

Comparison with prior radiographs is the most useful first step: a solid nodule unchanged for about two years is generally considered benign. Nipple shadows, skin lesions and overlapping bones can mimic a nodule on a single frontal view.

An indeterminate nodule is usually characterised with chest CT. Follow-up of nodules found on CT depends on their size, type and the patient's risk factors, following recommendations such as those of the Fleischner Society.
