# Atelectasis
fr: Atélectasie
aliases: lung collapse, volume loss, plate-like atelectasis, subsegmental atelectasis
source: https://radiopaedia.org/articles/atelectasis

Atelectasis is incomplete expansion of part of the lung. On a radiograph it combines increased opacity with signs of volume loss: displacement of a fissure, elevation of the hemidiaphragm, shift of the mediastinum or hilum towards the affected side, and crowding of vessels.

Linear or plate-like (subsegmental) atelectasis at the lung bases is common after surgery, in bedridden patients and with shallow breathing, and often resolves with mobilisation.

Collapse of a whole lobe in an adult should raise the possibility of an obstructing lesion in the airway, such as a mucus plug or a tumour; further imaging such as CT or bronchoscopy may be needed when it persists.
